Privacy Policy — Pizza Converter

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Pizza Converter ("the App" or "the Software") is developed by Ken Dao / NEEFLOW ("we", "us", or "our"). Your privacy is fundamental to our software design. This policy explains how Pizza Converter handles your data.

1. 100% Local On-Device Processing & Zero Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All file conversions, media encoding, image processing, OCR, and AI background removal happen 100% locally on your computer. Your documents, photos, audio, and video files never leave your device.

2. No Accounts, No Telemetry, No Tracking

The App requires no account registration, no email sign-in, and embeds zero third-party advertising SDKs, telemetry frameworks, or analytics trackers. We do not track what files you convert, how often you use the app, or your hardware identifiers.

3. File and Keyboard Input Permissions

  • File System Access: The App accesses local files solely when you explicitly select them for conversion or designate an output destination directory. No files are accessed in the background without user initiation.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: The App registers a local keyboard hook (default: Ctrl + G or hold Shift) exclusively to display the radial wheel menu at your mouse cursor. Keystrokes are never recorded, logged, or transmitted.

4. Local Storage Only

The App stores only functional preferences locally on your device (selected language, output folder path, custom hotkey, radial layout order, and conversion history for the Undo feature). This configuration data stays strictly on your computer and can be cleared at any time from Settings.

5. Third-Party Components & Runtimes

  • FFmpeg (bundled, LGPL-licensed) runs locally via command-line invocation to convert audio and video files. It initiates no network connections.
  • LibreOffice (optional, installed separately by you) runs locally to convert office documents. It sends no data anywhere.
  • On-Device Machine Learning Models (downloaded locally for background removal) execute directly via local CPU/GPU memory. Images are never uploaded to cloud AI APIs.

6. Pro Purchases & Store Commerce

Optional Pro upgrades are processed exclusively through official application stores (such as Microsoft Store or Apple App Store) under their respective security and privacy standards. NEEFLOW does not collect, receive, or store your credit card or billing details.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Because the App collects no data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with COPPA and does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at: privacy@neeflow.com or support@neeflow.com
