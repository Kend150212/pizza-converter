CORE ARCHITECTURE & ENGINE
⚡ High-Performance Rust & Tauri v2

Pizza Converter is engineered from the ground up with Tauri v2 and an asynchronous, multi-threaded Rust core engine. Conversions leverage direct memory streams, SIMD-accelerated image decoding, and isolated worker processes to deliver instantaneous conversions with minimal system memory footprint.

🔒 On-Device Intelligence & Zero Cloud

All intelligent features — including neural background removal, adaptive scan enhancement, document structure extraction, and PDF security manipulation — execute 100% locally on your machine. Your documents, photos, audio, or video files never leave your device.

PRIVACY POLICY
🛡 1. 100% Local On-Device Processing

Your privacy is fundamental to Pizza Converter. The application operates entirely offline on your local computer. We do not operate conversion servers, cloud relays, web proxies, or remote document processing systems. Every conversion, encoding, and AI task takes place strictly within your device's CPU and RAM.

🚫 2. Zero Telemetry & No Tracking

Pizza Converter requires no account registration, no login, and collects zero personal data. The app contains no third-party analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel), no advertising SDKs, and no background reporting mechanisms. We never know what files you convert, when you convert them, or your hardware identity.

📂 3. Transparent File & Keyboard Permissions

To provide seamless productivity, Pizza Converter utilizes standard OS system capabilities:

  • File System Access: Read and write permissions are used exclusively to process the specific files you select for conversion and save the outputs to your chosen folder. The app never scans or indexes your drive in the background.
  • Global Shortcuts: The keyboard hook (default: Ctrl + G or Shift) is used solely to detect the trigger event and display the radial menu at your cursor position. Keystrokes are never recorded, logged, or stored.
🧠 4. On-Device AI Background Removal

When utilizing AI features (such as one-click background removal or document deskewing), neural network inference runs 100% locally via embedded runtimes. No photos or visual contents are ever transmitted across the internet.

💳 5. Secure Commerce & Store In-App Purchases

Pro license purchases are handled directly through official application store commerce platforms (Microsoft Store / Apple App Store). NEEFLOW never collects, receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers or billing details.

⚖️ 6. Compliance & Contact

Because no personal data is collected, Pizza Converter complies naturally with European GDPR, California CCPA, and COPPA requirements. For privacy inquiries, contact us at: privacy@neeflow.com
